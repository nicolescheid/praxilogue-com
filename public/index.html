02

How I think about it

Most AI rollouts follow the same pattern: licences, an intro session, a prompt library, and an expectation that the rest will follow. It's an understandable assumption. AI works in plain English, so how hard can it be?

Harder than it looks. AI doesn't take instruction the way a colleague does, and a single quick request gets you an average answer. Output that rises above mediocre comes from working with it skilfully: framing the problem well, bringing your own experience to it, and knowing when to push back.

In a world where everyone has AI, your people's talent is still what makes the difference.

Tools follow the problem.

I work with your people to find the pain points worth solving, then help shape the tools that solve them. No AI for the sake of AI.

  • Fail small.
  • Delegate on purpose.
  • You can only prompt as deep as you know.
